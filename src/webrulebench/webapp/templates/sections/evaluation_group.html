Cross check nasıl okunur?

  • ⚠ Şüpheli: modeller birbirine çok uyuyor (≥ 0.9) ama GT skoru düşük (< 0.3) — ya modeller aynı hatayı yapıyor ya da GT'de sorun var; GT'yi kontrol edin.
  • 🧩 Zor: modeller birbirinden ayrışıyor (uyum < 0.3) — iskelette ayırt edici işaret az olan alanlar.
  • İki modelin de boş döndürdüğü hücreler hesaba katılmaz.

Tabs

  • Ranking: each model × rule language experiment; mean, generalization (outside the sample page), MATCH rate, errors, tokens and time. Click a row to open the experiment's details.
  • Heat map: model × field mean — which model is good at which field.
  • Per layout: the models' mean on each layout; the best one is highlighted, win counts are at the bottom.
  • Cross check: agreement of the contents that the models extract on the same page and field with each other (mean pairwise ROUGE-1 F1 / Jaccard / exact match, independent of the GT). The rules are run again; no LLM calls are made.

How to read the cross check

  • ⚠ Suspicious: the models agree strongly (≥ 0.9) but the GT score is low (< 0.3) — either the models make the same mistake or there is a problem in the GT; check the GT.
  • 🧩 Hard: the models diverge from each other (agreement < 0.3) — fields with few distinctive markers in the skeleton.
  • Cells where both models returned nothing are not counted.
