Usage

  1. Each row is a layout. The CSS, XPath and Regex columns show the GT status of that rule language.
  2. Go to the compilation page with ⚙ Compile (admin) or 👁 Review.

Columns

  • Annotators: users who have assigned pages to this layout or entered rules for it.
  • Pages: number of pages assigned to this layout in the GT / union of the pages assigned by the annotators.
  • Rule language cell: status, number of annotators who entered a rule, and the mean rule agreement — the share of annotator pairs that wrote the same (normalized) rule (if there are at least two annotators).

Statuses

  • ✓ approved GT exists and nothing has changed since the compilation.
  • ⚠ has updates At least one annotator has changed their rules since the compilation.
  • ○ no GT No GT has been created for this language.

On the compilation screen CSS, XPath and Regex have separate tabs; the admin chooses which rule languages are included in the GT. The approval time is kept per language.
